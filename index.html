Biophysical profile

Manning ultrasound BPP: 2 points if the component is present, 0 if absent. NST is optional (modified BPP = NST + amniotic fluid). Fluid can be taken from the Anomaly → Amniotic fluid entry.

A score of 8/8 or 10/10 is reassuring when fluid is normal. 6/10 is equivocal. ≤4/10 is abnormal. An abnormal modified BPP is a non-reactive NST or oligohydramnios, even if the other component is normal. Decision support only — timing of delivery is a clinical decision.

Interpretation rules (ranges, not single cut-offs)

Follicle size at ovulation varies between people and cycles. Size alone does not prove ovulation. Labels below are decision-support categories for structured reporting.

LabelTypical ultrasound pattern
BaselineEarly cycle (often CD 2–5): cohort usually <10 mm; endometrium thin / menstrual.
Developing follicleGrowing cohort; no clearly leading follicle, or leader still <10 mm.
Dominant follicleLeading follicle about 10–16 mm and clearly ahead of the cohort.
Pre-ovulatoryNatural cycles: leading mean diameter often ~18–25 mm with a smooth wall; endometrium commonly trilaminar and often ≥7–8 mm (range, not a hard cut-off). Stimulated cycles: trigger size is protocol-dependent, often ~16–22 mm.
Peri-ovulatoryMature-range follicle plus crenation or internal echoes without definite collapse.
Probable ovulationTwo independent signs (collapse or ≥30% size drop, crenation, new internal echoes, new POD fluid, corpus luteum, trilaminar→secretory shift).
Consistent with ovulationThree or more signs, preferably including follicular collapse or a typical corpus luteum.
Luteal phaseCorpus luteum ± secretory endometrium after the expected ovulatory window.
Possible LUFS / unrupturedPersistent mature-range follicle past the expected window, without collapse, sometimes with luteinised wall or secretory endometrium.
IndeterminateSerial imaging insufficient; do not over-call ovulation.

Growth rate: natural cycles often ~1.0–2.0 mm/day; stimulated cycles may be faster (~1.5–3 mm/day). Stagnation is suggested if growth is <0.5 mm/day over ≥48 hours while still in the follicular phase. A corpus luteum is typically a thick-walled cystic structure with peripheral (“ring of fire”) vascularity; it is not a diagnosis of pregnancy.

How to use these citations

Every formula and threshold in Babsinogen is mapped to a published source. Prefer the primary paper or society statement when counselling or writing a formal report. This page is the bibliography; the calculators remain decision support, not a textbook.

Dating & EDD

  • Naegele’s rule. Classic EDD = LMP + 280 days (28-day cycle, ovulation day 14). Cycle-adjusted: EDD = LMP + cycle − luteal + 266.
  • CRL. Robinson HP, Fleming JEE. Br J Obstet Gynaecol 1975. Corrected form: GA(days) = 8.052√(CRL×1.037) + 23.73.
  • MSD. Hellman/Kobayashi: GA(days) ≈ MSD(mm) + 30, only if CRL is not measurable. MSD ≥25 mm without an embryo raises concern for early pregnancy failure.
  • Redating. ACOG/AIUM/SMFM Committee Opinion 700 (2017): ≤8w6d >5 days; 9w0d–13w6d >7 days; 14–15w6d >7 days; 16–21w6d >10 days; 22–27w6d >14 days; ≥28 weeks >21 days.
  • Later dating rank. Chervenak et al., AJR 1991 — shape-corrected BPD and HC (plus FL in the third trimester) outperform AC for dating.
  • FHR. Doubilet & Benson, AJR 1995. Peak near 9–10 weeks; 110–160 bpm from about 14 weeks. Ibrahim et al., J Perinat Med 2022.

Biometry, EFW & growth

  • HC / cBPD. HC = 1.62×(BPD+OFD); cBPD = √[(BPD×OFD)/1.265], Doubilet & Greenes, AJR 1984 — dating only, not EFW.
  • Cephalic index. Hadlock et al., AJR 1981: 74–83 (mean 78.3).
  • EFW Hadlock I–IV. Hadlock et al., AJOG 1985;151:333–337. Related FL paper: Radiology 1984;150:535–540.
  • GA from biometry. Hadlock et al., Radiology 1984.
  • Centiles 24–41w. Hadlock 1991 with Gardosi/Mikolajczyk SD methodology (Lancet 2011).
  • SGA/LGA vs macrosomia. ACOG: SGA <10th (severe <3rd); LGA ≥90th. Macrosomia is absolute weight (PB 216): ≥4000 / 4500 / 5000 g.
  • Ratios. HC/AC (Divon, AAFP 1998); FL/AC ~20–24% after ~21 weeks (Hiersch, AJOG 2018); early BPD/FL (Wang 2017).

Fetal Doppler

  • UA-PI. INTERGROWTH-21st LMS, Drukker et al., AJOG 2020 Table 6. AEDF/REDF remain absolute findings.
  • MCA-PSV. Mari et al., NEJM 2000: median e^(2.31+0.046×GA); 1.5 MoM anaemia threshold. False positives rise after ~35 weeks.
  • CPR. MCA-PI ÷ UA-PI; <1.0 qualitative brain-sparing flag.
  • Uterine artery. 95th ~2.35 in the first trimester, ~1.60 at 18–24 weeks. Notching may still be physiological before 24 weeks.
  • Ductus venosus. PI declines ~0.57 to 0.44 from 21–40 weeks (Bahlmann). Absent/reversed a-wave is the higher-yield flag.

Anomaly markers

  • Ventriculomegaly. Atrium 10–12 mm mild, 13–15 moderate, >15 severe.
  • Posterior fossa. Dandy–Walker vs Chiari II; TCD (Kotgirwar 2021). 4th ventricle–cisterna magna communication can be normal before 20 weeks.
  • NT / nasal bone / nuchal fold. Cicero et al.; ACOG/SMFM nuchal fold ≥6 mm. NT 3.5 mm is a simplified cutoff.
  • CTR. Circumference method <0.5; area method ~0.25–0.35 — do not mix.
  • Skeletal. Chest/AC <0.83 with a narrow thorax raises pulmonary-hypoplasia concern. FL/foot <0.87 vs isolated FGR.
  • Renal pelvis. GA-banded (e.g. 5 mm at 21–30 weeks); not asserted beyond 30 weeks.
  • Fluid. ACOG prefers SDP <2 cm for oligohydramnios. Typical AFI 5–24 cm.
  • Placenta. Thickness often flagged >40 mm in the second trimester, >60 mm in the third.

Pregnancy impression, EPL, IUFD & BPP

  • SRU early pregnancy (2012, 2024 update). Definitive TV criteria: CRL ≥7 mm without cardiac activity; MSD ≥25 mm without an embryo; no embryo with heartbeat ≥14 days after a GS without a yolk sac, or ≥11 days after a GS with a yolk sac.
  • Suspicious, not diagnostic. CRL <7 mm no FCA; MSD 16–24 mm no embryo; yolk sac >6 mm; slow early FHR (Doubilet & Benson).
  • IUFD. Death at ≥20 weeks: absent cardiac activity and absent movement on ultrasound (not handheld Doppler/CTG alone). Spalding/maceration/gas are supportive.
  • FGR / SGA. SGA: EFW <10th. FGR: EFW <3rd, or <10th with Doppler/fluid/small-AC features (ISUOG / ACOG OCC).
  • Fluid. Oligohydramnios: SDP <2 cm or AFI ≤5 cm (ACOG prefers SDP). Polyhydramnios: SDP ≥8 cm or AFI ≥24–25 cm.
  • BPP. Manning 2/0 scoring. 8/8 or 10/10 reassuring if fluid is normal; 6/10 equivocal (repeat); ≤4/10 abnormal. Modified BPP = NST + fluid; either component abnormal = abnormal modified BPP.

Folliculometry

  • Nomenclature. NICHD ovarian follicle workshop 2024 — dominant antral ~10–15 mm; pre-ovulatory often 16–28 mm.
  • Natural growth. About 1.0–1.4 mm/day to a mean ~20–24 mm at ovulation (range). Stimulated cycles may be faster (~1.7–2.0 mm/day). StatPearls.
  • Trigger size. Protocol-dependent (often ~18–20 mm gonadotropin/IUI; clomiphene commonly larger). Not a universal cutoff.
  • Ovulation signs. Collapse, crenation, internal echoes, pouch-of-Douglas fluid, corpus luteum (thick wall ± ring of fire), endometrial shift. Size or calendar day alone is insufficient.
  • Endometrium. Trilaminar peri-ovulatory pattern; <7 mm often called thin in ART series, not an absolute barrier.
  • Timing. Expected ovulation ≈ cycle length − luteal length (luteal typically 12–16 days). Do not assume 28 days.

Gynaecology & organ size

  • IOTA Simple Rules. Timmerman et al., BMJ 2008. Malignant = M-feature(s) and no B-feature; both or neither = inconclusive. ADNEX is not implemented.
  • Cyst follow-up. Levine et al., SRU consensus. Ovarian volume: Raffone/SIGO-SIE 2022. No single postmenopausal cutoff is asserted.
  • Endometrium. Postmenopausal bleeding often ≤5 mm reassuring; asymptomatic postmenopausal often ~≤11 mm; premenopausal by cycle phase.
  • Spleen. Rosenberg et al., AJR 1991. Kidney. Emamian et al., AJR 1993 (paediatric mean). Uterus. Kelsey et al., PLOS ONE 2016.
  • IPP. Grade I <5 mm, II 5–10 mm, III >10 mm (Lee, Korean J Urol 2013), bladder comfortably full.
  • Aorta. AAA ≥3 cm; elective-repair discussion commonly ≥5.5 cm. Portal vein ~13 mm.
  • Textbook viscera: Rumack & Levine, Diagnostic Ultrasound.
Scope notes

“Microsomia” is not standard terminology — use SGA or FGR. AEDF/REDF override UA-PI numbers. CPR <1.0 is a qualitative brain-sparing flag. Paediatric kidney values are means, not pass/fail cutoffs. Recheck bladder wall on a distended bladder. Uterine-artery PI here is not MoM-adjusted. Folliculometry series stay in localStorage; the patient log uses IndexedDB and is off until per-episode consent is ticked. Nothing leaves this device. This app is not FDA-cleared. EFW error is typically ±10–15%. Doppler assumes angle correction <30°. Cycle-adjusted dating is used only when you enter cycle length.

Limitations & safety

  • International charts (Hadlock, INTERGROWTH-21st, SRU) may not match every local population.
  • TI-RADS, RMI, Bosniak, and IOTA classify a pattern — they are not a histopathology or FNA result.
  • Red-flag banners flag published criteria; they do not time delivery or replace an emergency call.
  • Play Store / Microsoft Store packaging is not part of this web PWA. Install from the browser (“Add to Home Screen”) for offline use.

RMI, TI-RADS, Bosniak, BI-RADS

  • RMI-I. Jacobs et al., Br J Obstet Gynaecol 1990; 97:922–9. U × M × CA-125. RCOG Green-top 34 commonly uses 200 as the specialist-referral cut-off.
  • ACR TI-RADS. Tessler et al., JACR 2017; 14:587–95. DOI 10.1016/j.jacr.2017.01.046. Points for composition, echogenicity, shape, margin, echogenic foci; TR1–TR5 size thresholds for FNA vs follow-up.
  • Bosniak 2019. Silverman et al., Radiology 2019; 292:475–80 (renal cystic masses). Categories I–IV are entered as the published class; this tool does not invent a point score.
  • ACR BI-RADS Atlas 5th Edition (2013), ultrasound. Lexicon (shape, orientation, margin, echo, posterior features, special cases) plus an assessment category the reader assigns. Not a point score. Category 3 <2% malignancy; 5 ≥95%.
  • Scan-week windows. ISUOG first-trimester and midtrimester anatomy practice guidelines; FMF NT 11+0–13+6 (CRL 45–84 mm); SRU 2012/2024 early-pregnancy diagnostic criteria; ACOG Committee Opinion 700 redating; IUFD ≥20 weeks as used elsewhere in this app.
