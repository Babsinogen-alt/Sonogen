Interpretation rules (ranges, not single cut-offs)

Follicle size at ovulation varies between people and cycles. Size alone does not prove ovulation. Labels below are decision-support categories for structured reporting.

LabelTypical ultrasound pattern
BaselineEarly cycle (often CD 2–5): cohort usually <10 mm; endometrium thin / menstrual.
Developing follicleGrowing cohort; no clearly leading follicle, or leader still <10 mm.
Dominant follicleLeading follicle about 10–16 mm and clearly ahead of the cohort.
Pre-ovulatoryNatural cycles: leading mean diameter often ~18–25 mm with a smooth wall; endometrium commonly trilaminar and often ≥7–8 mm (range, not a hard cut-off). Stimulated cycles: trigger size is protocol-dependent, often ~16–22 mm.
Peri-ovulatoryMature-range follicle plus crenation or internal echoes without definite collapse.
Probable ovulationTwo independent signs (collapse or ≥30% size drop, crenation, new internal echoes, new POD fluid, corpus luteum, trilaminar→secretory shift).
Consistent with ovulationThree or more signs, preferably including follicular collapse or a typical corpus luteum.
Luteal phaseCorpus luteum ± secretory endometrium after the expected ovulatory window.
Possible LUFS / unrupturedPersistent mature-range follicle past the expected window, without collapse, sometimes with luteinised wall or secretory endometrium.
IndeterminateSerial imaging insufficient; do not over-call ovulation.

Growth rate: natural cycles often ~1.0–2.0 mm/day; stimulated cycles may be faster (~1.5–3 mm/day). Stagnation is suggested if growth is <0.5 mm/day over ≥48 hours while still in the follicular phase. A corpus luteum is typically a thick-walled cystic structure with peripheral (“ring of fire”) vascularity; it is not a diagnosis of pregnancy.

Scope notes

"Microsomia" is not standard fetal-medicine terminology — undergrowth is described as SGA (a size descriptor) or FGR (a pathological process); the two are not synonymous, and distinguishing them needs serial growth and Doppler, not a single EFW. UA-PI is compared with INTERGROWTH-21st 5th/50th/95th centiles (Drukker 2020 LMS model). Still prioritise AEDF/REDF, which are absolute findings. CPR <1.0 (MCA-PI < UA-PI) is the standard qualitative brain-sparing flag. Kidney paediatric reference is a population mean (for comparison), not an upper-limit cutoff like the other age-graded tables. Hepatic artery is assessed here by resistive index, not diameter, since it has no standard reference calibre the way the portal/splenic/hepatic veins do. Bladder wall thickness depends on distension — always re-check on a distended bladder before calling a thickened wall abnormal. Endometrial thickness is context-dependent (menopausal status, symptoms, cycle phase) — the tool asks which context applies rather than using one fixed number. Uterine artery PI is shown against a fixed 95th-centile figure, not a MoM adjusted for maternal ethnicity, weight, or height, which is the more precise approach where available. Ovarian cysts get two separate assessments: a quick size/follow-up-band descriptor, and the full IOTA Simple Rules (all 10 features, the published decision algorithm). The ADNEX model is deliberately not implemented — it's a multi-coefficient logistic regression requiring a serum CA-125 value and precise published coefficients across five outcome categories, and a hand-built version of that carries more risk of a subtle, hard-to-catch error than the benefit justifies here. For an inconclusive Simple Rules result, or for a formal percentage risk, use expert ultrasound assessment or the official ADNEX calculator directly. Postmenopausal ovarian volume has no single agreed cutoff in the literature and none is asserted here. Folliculometry series are stored only on this device (localStorage) and can be cleared at any time. Patient Log is entirely local to this device (IndexedDB) and off by default — nothing is saved unless the consent checkbox is ticked for that specific episode, and every episode can be deleted individually or all at once. There is no account, no sync, and no network call anywhere in this app; closing the browser tab discards anything not explicitly saved.
