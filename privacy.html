Sonogen privacy

Last updated 2 November 2026. This page is the public statement for the website and the Android app.

What Sonogen is

Sonogen is a calculator and reporting aid for qualified health professionals. It is decision support. It is not a medical device, and it is not cleared or approved by NAFDAC, the FDA, or a CE body.

What stays on this device

Anything you type stays in this app on this device. That includes names, identification you choose to enter, measurements, folliculometry series, examination notes, quiz progress, and display settings. The calculators run on the device. There is no account.

The examination log uses the device’s own storage and stays off until you tick consent for that episode. Folders and settings use the device’s local storage.

What leaves this device

Nothing is uploaded to the developer. There is no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking cookie. Report-an-error text is copied on the device. It is not sent.

The Android app does not use Android backup, so those records are not copied to a Google account by Sonogen. A phone browser can still back up a website if you have turned that on in the browser. That is the browser, not Sonogen.

If you export a file or print a report, that copy is yours. Sonogen does not receive it.

How to delete it

In the browser, clear the site data for this address. In the Android app, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall it. Either one deletes the saved names, numbers, and examinations on that device. Export a backup first if you still need them.

Children

Sonogen is not made for children. It is a professional tool.

Contact

Questions about this statement can be sent through the Sonogen repository. The developer does not operate a separate data service, because the app does not receive the records.
